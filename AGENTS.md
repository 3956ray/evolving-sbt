# Project verification

For verifying Evolving SBT behavior or maintaining its Feature Map, read `.agents/skills/verify-evolving-sbt/SKILL.md` and the selected feature recipe. Keep simulation, live local RPC and public-network claims distinct. Preserve action/result evidence after cleanup. Expected behavior comes from explicit requirements and the implementation specification, not automatically from current code. Verification-only tasks do not authorize product changes, public deployments, pushes or merges.
